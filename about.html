About The Company

WE BELIEVE NOURISHING HOME MEALS SHOULD BE EFFORTLESS.

Bridging the gap between busy modern routines and genuine homestyle food.

FROZIO is a modern ready-to-cook frozen food brand founded on a simple insight: families want wholesome, authentic, and delicious meals without spending hours peeling, chopping, kneading, or cleaning up. Our products go straight from your freezer to your tawa, pan, or air-fryer in 5 to 10 minutes—delivering piping-hot golden crunch, flaky ribbon layers, and peak garden-fresh tenderness every single time.

Farm-Direct Sourcing Tender green peas, sweet corn, and garden vegetables harvested at peak ripeness and processed within hours.
Master Chef Formulations Authentic regional seasonings crafted by culinary experts so every samosa, paratha, and cutlet tastes freshly handmade.
Clean Food Standards Zero chemical preservatives, zero trans fats, and zero artificial dyes. Certified FSSAI and HACCP compliant processing.
Certified Food Safety Standards Hygienic culinary kitchens locking in authentic flavor and nutrition.
Heritage Meets Modern Science Traditional artisan techniques preserved with sub-zero freezing.
Company History

FROM A FAMILY KITCHEN TO A NATIONWIDE PANTRY ESSENTIAL.

Our journey started with a cherished grandmother's recipe for flaky, ribbon-layered parathas. We refused to believe that convenient frozen food had to taste artificial or compromise on health.

Over the years, we combined authentic home-kitchen craftsmanship with Individual Quick Freezing (-40°C) and unbroken sub-zero cold-chain logistics—growing step by step into a pantry favourite across India.

FROZIO Pillars

FOUR CORE COMMITMENTS BEHIND EVERY PACK.

Our quality standards are uncompromising. From raw vegetable harvesting to your home freezer, every step is rigorously governed by our four founding pillars.

100% Honest Ingredients

Pure stone-ground wheat, hand-selected vegetables, and clean meats. Zero synthetic colors, zero MSG, and zero chemical preservatives.

IQF Flash Freezing

Individual Quick Freezing locks food at -40°C in minutes, preserving cell structure so textures remain crisp and never soggy upon heating.

Monitored Cold Chain

Our insulated delivery fleet is temperature-monitored 24/7 to ensure zero transit thawing or mid-delivery temperature fluctuations.

Effortless Zero Prep

Skip washing, chopping, and dough kneading. Cook straight from frozen to table in minutes with maximum flavor and minimal kitchen mess.
